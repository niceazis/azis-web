The problem

Important work gets lost inside conversations.

Teams make decisions and assign work across chat, email, meetings, and documents. The information exists, but the next action is often unclear or forgotten.

01

Ownership is unclear

Requests are discussed, but responsibility is not always captured explicitly.

02

Deadlines disappear

Due dates are buried in threads and notes instead of staying visible.

03

Follow-up is manual

Teams rely on memory to track open decisions, dependencies, and risks.

Product

Turn unstructured work into a clear action layer.

AZIS is designed to transform everyday work signals into structured information that teams can review, track, and act on.

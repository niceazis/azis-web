About

Software built around practical automation.

Founded in 2026, AZIS is an independent software startup based in South Korea. Our work explores how AI can support research, coding, summarization, task analysis, knowledge management, and multi-step operational workflows.

What we do

Three areas of focus.

Designing multi-step workflows that help move work from input to a structured outcome rather than stopping at a single chat response.

Agents · Orchestration

Knowledge automation
